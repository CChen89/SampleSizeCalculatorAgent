## 🎓 Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific about effect sizes** - this is the most critical parameter
2. **Include realistic dropout estimates** - affects final sample size significantly  
3. **Specify power requirements clearly** - 80% vs 90% makes a big difference
4. **Describe endpoint relationships** - for multiple endpoint studies
5. **Mention any special constraints** - budget, time, recruitment considerations
